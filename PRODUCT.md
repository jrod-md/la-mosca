# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People in Panama who follow the Liga Panameña de Fútbol and the national team, plus a broader
internet audience that enjoys "a fly brain does X" experiments (the Beat Saber fly, the blackjack
fly, Stonkfly). They arrive from a shared link or a social post, usually on a phone, often during
the weekend fixture window, and stay for a minute or two: who did the fly bet on, did it win,
how broke is it now. Returning visitors check the record after each round.

## Product Purpose

A simulated fly brain (real MaleCNS mushroom body connectivity, 296 neurons) bets a fake
bankroll on real Panamanian football. Fixtures, results and prices are real or modeled from real
data; the money is not. Every bet is committed to a public git history before kickoff, so the
record is honest and verifiable. Success: people share the fly's picks, argue with a fly, and come
back to see whether it went broke.

## Brand Personality

Deadpan, nerdy, affectionate. The fly is the protagonist and a character with quirks (it was born
loving Independiente and fearing Tauro), never a lab subject or a victim. Humor comes from taking
the fly completely seriously as a bettor. Science is shown, not explained: real neurons lighting
up, real synapse counts, with clear labels on what is real and what is modeled.

## Anti-references

- Real sportsbooks and casinos (green felt, gold, "BET NOW", bonus banners). This is not gambling
  and must never read as an invitation to gamble.
- Crypto/trading neon-on-black dashboards.
- The previous MetroFly framing: specimen IDs, lab-subject copy, "suffering" meters. Nothing that
  makes the fly look trapped or mistreated.
- Generic AI dashboards: hero-metric tiles, identical card grids, gradient text.

## Design Principles

1. The fly is the star. The 3D scene and its brain lead; numbers support the story.
2. Honest by construction. Real vs modeled is always labeled; fake money is unmistakably fake.
3. One glance, one story. Within seconds a visitor knows the current pick, the bankroll and the
   fly's mood.
4. Panama-local, internet-legible. Spanish first with English available; club names and places as
   fans say them.
5. Marea Roja is an event. National team matches transform the whole room, not just an accent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA for text and controls. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support (scene holds still,
brain shows static activity). The 3D scene is decorative; every fact it shows (pick, odds, stake,
bankroll, result) is also available as text. Color is never the only signal for won/lost.
